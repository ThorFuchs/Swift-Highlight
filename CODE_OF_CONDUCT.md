# Community standards

Swift-Highlight welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and levels
of experience. Make technical discussions specific, constructive and respectful.
Critique ideas without attacking people, explain assumptions, and protect
credentials, private data, names and accessibility needs. Harassment,
discrimination, threats and unwanted contact are not acceptable.

Maintainers may use GitHub's moderation tools to address disruptive behavior.
For a public moderation concern, open an issue without repeating sensitive
details; use GitHub's report-abuse controls for private reports. Vulnerability
reports follow the separate process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
